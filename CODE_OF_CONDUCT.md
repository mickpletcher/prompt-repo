# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Contributors and maintainers are expected to be respectful, constructive, and
focused on improving the shared resource. Examples of positive participation
include:

- giving specific, actionable feedback
- respecting different experience levels and viewpoints
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them
- protecting private information and credentials
- prioritizing community safety over personal advantage

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption,
publishing another person's private information, and other unprofessional
conduct are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
spaces where someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method on the maintainer's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/mickpletcher). Do not include sensitive
personal information in a public issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful or inappropriate.
Reports will be reviewed as fairly and privately as practical.
